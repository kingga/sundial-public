Sundial Support

Questions, problems or ideas? Email contact.sundial.app@gmail.com with your Sundial version (shown under About), your device model and its system version. Please don't send event titles or photos unless they're needed to explain the problem.

You can also report a bug or suggest a feature at github.com/kingga/sundial-public/issues, if you have a GitHub account. Everything posted there is public, so email for anything private.

Questions

Where are my events stored?

Only on your device. Sundial has no account or cloud sync.

How do I move my events to a new phone?

They move with your device's backup or transfer (iCloud Backup or Quick Start on iPhone; Google backup or phone-to-phone transfer on Android), if Sundial is included in it.

What does archiving do?

It moves an event to Archived, out of the way. Open Archived with the box icon at the top of the home screen; you can restore or delete the event there.

Can I get back an event I deleted?

Tap Undo, which shows for a few seconds after you delete. After that, Sundial can't bring it back.

Why isn't my widget up to date?

Your device decides when widgets refresh. On Android, allowing Alarms & reminders helps widgets for timed events change on time. If a widget still looks wrong, open Sundial.

What happens if I delete Sundial?

Its events and photos are removed from your device. Backups you've already made may still contain them, depending on your settings.

Reminders

When do Reminders come?

Remind me is on for every new event that counts down. Sundial reminds you on the day: at 8:00 am wherever you are for an all-day event, or at the moment itself for a timed event. You can add one Early Reminder too: 1 day, 2 days or 1 week before an all-day event, or 15 minutes, 1 hour, 1 day or 1 week before a timed event. Events that count up from a past date have no Reminders.

How do I turn Reminders off?

For one event, edit it and turn off Remind me. Archiving or deleting an event also cancels its Reminders. To stop them all, turn off notifications for Sundial in your device's settings: on iPhone, Settings, then Notifications, then Sundial; on Android, Sundial's app info, then Notifications.

Why didn't a Reminder arrive?

  • Check that Sundial may send notifications. If you said no when Sundial asked, edit an event that counts down and tap Open Settings under "Notifications are off for Sundial". Once you turn notifications on and go back to Sundial, your Reminders come back without editing anything.
  • On Android, allow Alarms & reminders for Sundial (in Settings, under special app access) so Reminders arrive on time. Without it, Android may show one up to about an hour late.
  • Do Not Disturb, a Focus or a notification summary can hold Reminders back or silence them. Allow Sundial in it if you want them straight away.
  • On Android, battery settings that restrict Sundial can delay Reminders. If you force stop Sundial, Android cancels its Reminders until you open Sundial again.
  • A Reminder whose time had already passed when you saved the event isn't shown.
  • Opening Sundial sets up its Reminders again.

Sundial Pro

What does Sundial Pro add?

Free Sundial keeps up to 3 events, with every other feature, widgets included; archived events don't count. Sundial Pro is a single purchase that lets you keep as many as you like. See the Terms of Use for details.

Can I try Pro first?

On iPhone, yes: once, free, for 3 days. Open About in Sundial, tap Try Pro free, read what the Trial is, then tap Try Pro free again. It's also offered when you try to add a fourth event. The days left show in About. Android has no Trial.

What happens when the Trial ends?

Every event and widget keeps working, and nothing is deleted or charged. You can't add or restore events until you have fewer than 3 that aren't archived, unless you buy Pro. Reinstalling Sundial doesn't start the Trial again.

How do I get Pro back on a new phone?

Sign in with the same Apple ID or Google account you bought it with, and open Sundial: Pro usually comes back by itself. If it doesn't, open About and tap Restore Purchases.

My purchase says "Waiting for approval"

The purchase is waiting for a parent or family organiser to approve it or for a payment to clear. Pro, or the Trial if that's what you were starting, turns on by itself once it does; you don't need to keep Sundial open.

How do I redeem a code?

On iPhone, open About in Sundial and tap Redeem code. On Android, redeem it in the Google Play Store app: tap your profile picture, then Payments & subscriptions, then Redeem code. If Pro isn't on when you go back to Sundial, open About and tap Restore Purchases.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store you bought from. On iPhone, use Apple's Report a Problem page. On Android, see Google Play's refund help. Your events stay as they are either way.
